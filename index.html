Agen Misteri

Personal tool for creating and uploading short mystery videos / Alat pribadi untuk membuat dan mengunggah video misteri pendek

What the app does

Agen Misteri is a personal, non-commercial automation tool. It writes a short mystery script, generates narration and illustrations, edits a vertical video (9:16), and then uploads the finished video as a private video to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API (youtube.upload permission). The owner then reviews the video and decides whether to publish it.

  • The app is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account and YouTube channel.
  • It does not read, list, edit or delete existing videos, comments, subscribers or analytics.
  • It does not collect or share data about any other person.

Apa yang dilakukan aplikasi ini

Agen Misteri adalah alat otomasi pribadi (non-komersial) yang membuat video misteri pendek lalu mengunggahnya sebagai video private ke kanal YouTube milik pemiliknya sendiri. Pemilik memeriksa video terlebih dahulu sebelum memutuskan untuk mempublikasikannya.
